# AFAAQ ARAB — Product & Messaging Source of Truth

## Product role

This repository is the public website for **AFAAQ ARAB for Engineering & Contracting**, a Cairo-based electrical engineering and contracting company.

The website is a commercial engineering-credibility surface. Its job is not to behave like a startup landing page, design portfolio, or generic corporate brochure.

## Primary audiences

The website should be immediately useful to:

- Utility project managers
- EPC project managers
- Engineering consultants
- Protection & control engineers
- Procurement stakeholders
- OEM and electrical-infrastructure partners

## Primary commercial job

A qualified visitor should be able to determine quickly:

1. What AFAAQ does.
2. Which power-system disciplines AFAAQ covers.
3. What verified project experience exists.
4. What delivery scale the company can prove.
5. Which organizations appear in AFAAQ's project/client relationships.
6. How to send a technical project requirement.

## Positioning

AFAAQ should read as an **established electrical engineering and contracting company** focused on field execution for power systems.

The public positioning is grounded in the verified content source:

- Electrical installation
- Testing & commissioning
- Protection & control
- Power quality and energy solutions
- Operation & maintenance
- Engineering support
- Technical training

Company facts currently represented in the site source include:

- Founded: 2017
- Location: Cairo, Egypt
- 150+ substations delivered
- 50+ engineers & technicians
- 60% of EETC control centers project experience

Do not strengthen, reinterpret, globalize, or expand these claims without explicit user approval and source verification.

## Project truth

The current website project references are structured records, not a photography portfolio.

The verified project source is `src/content/projects.ts`.

Rules:

- Never invent a project.
- Never invent a client relationship.
- Never invent a voltage level, year, location, scope, system, or project result.
- Never use stock or unrelated photography as if it depicts a named AFAAQ project.
- If authentic project photography is not verified, present the project through factual structured data.

## Messaging principles

### Be specific

Prefer concrete engineering nouns and actions:

- protection relay testing
- secondary injection testing
- control circuit verification
- interlocking checks
- RTU integration
- commissioning
- energization support
- switchgear and panels
- AC/DC auxiliary systems

Avoid vague phrases such as:

- advanced solutions
- world-class excellence
- innovative engineering solutions
- industry-leading expertise
- cutting-edge technology

unless a specific verified fact supports the phrase.

### Write for technical buyers

Copy should help utility/EPC/engineering readers understand scope, evidence, and delivery—not explain the website layout or praise the brand.

Do not publish design-rationale sentences such as why one metric was separated from another.

### Use active, factual language

Prefer:

> AFAAQ tests protection relays, control circuits and interlocking systems.

over:

> AFAAQ provides best-in-class innovative protection solutions.

## Site hierarchy

The Home page should answer, in order:

1. Who AFAAQ is.
2. What technical work AFAAQ does.
3. What projects prove it.
4. What scale the company can demonstrate.
5. Which project/client relationships exist.
6. How AFAAQ executes work.
7. What the company is institutionally.
8. How to start a project inquiry.

## What AFAAQ is not

The website must not visually or verbally position AFAAQ as:

- a software/SaaS startup
- a design studio
- an architecture portfolio
- a generic marketing agency
- a global OEM unless verified content explicitly supports that claim
- a project-photography portfolio when authentic project images are unavailable

## Benchmark category

Visual maturity may be benchmarked against established electrical and energy-technology companies for discipline, hierarchy, clarity and institutional confidence.

Do not copy another company's identity, typography, slogans, layout, product claims, or proprietary brand assets.

## Conversion

**Discuss / send a project scope**
